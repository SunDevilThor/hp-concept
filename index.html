The Trailer

Started out
hauling horses

Most coffee carts roll off a lot already looking like coffee carts. This one spent the 1980s as a horse trailer.

It was stripped to the frame and rebuilt into a working espresso bar, keeping the shape and the stance that make people look twice before they order. Nothing about it came off a shelf.

The Hitching Post Coffee Co is veteran owned, based in Casa Grande, and freshly open for business. Same trailer, brand new purpose.
